# Fact Rules

These are hard rules for this project. Do not ship app content that violates them.

## High-Risk Football Facts

### Ronaldo Nazario / R9
- R9 has 0 Champions League titles.
- Do not write that R9 has a Champions League title.
- It is acceptable to frame "no Champions League" as his biggest club-career regret.

### Ronaldinho
- 2005 Bernabeu Clasico: Ronaldinho scored 2 goals, not a hat trick.
- The match was Real Madrid 0-3 Barcelona; Samuel Eto'o scored the first goal.
- Do not write "小罗伯纳乌帽子戏法".

### Kaka
- Kaka was not a 2002 World Cup starter.
- He was a young substitute and only had a brief appearance against Costa Rica.
- Do not write "卡卡2002世界杯主力" or "出场5次".
- In 2006-07 Champions League, Kaka scored 10 goals across the full campaign.
- Do not write "欧冠淘汰赛10球"; use "欧冠全程10球金靴".

### Neymar
- Neymar did not participate in the 2019 Copa America after injury before the tournament.
- Do not count 2019 Copa America as Neymar's personal/core national-team honor.
- It can be used as an attack point: Brazil won it without him.

### Mbappe
- Mbappe joined Real Madrid in summer 2024.
- The transfer was a free transfer after leaving PSG, not a 2023 transfer fee record.
- Do not write "2023转会皇马" or "成为世界上身价最高的球员" for that move.

### Maradona
- Before 1995, the Ballon d'Or was limited to European players.
- Maradona had no eligibility for the 1986 Ballon d'Or.
- Do not write "马拉多纳1986金球奖第二".
- 1986 World Cup: use "全程5球5助攻" unless separately verifying knockout-only numbers.

### Pele
- Pele's 1283-goal figure is a broad-count figure and includes friendlies/exhibitions/other non-modern-official contexts.
- If using 1283 goals, label the counting口径 clearly.
- Do not compare 1283 directly with modern official-only goal totals without explanation.
- Pele's 1962 World Cup contribution was limited by injury; avoid writing as if he was the core of all three Brazil titles.

## Style Rules
- Punchlines can be aggressive but should not contain easily falsifiable facts.
- Use "球迷会这么喷" style only when the claim is subjective or framed as banter.
- Do not let meme phrasing override hard facts.

## Current / Moving Facts
Avoid exact current totals for active players unless verified in the same turn:
- Mbappe club and France goal totals.
- Haaland Norway and club goal totals.
- Ballon d'Or rankings after 2024.
- Current market values and transfer status.

If exact current data is necessary, verify from official or primary sources before writing.
